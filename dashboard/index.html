How the agent works

Every hour from 6:15 AM to 5:15 PM IST on weekdays, a data job on GitHub downloads the Nifty 500 list from NSE, plus prices and fundamentals from Yahoo Finance. It then scores every stock. About half an hour later, Claude runs in Anthropic's cloud. It reads the news for your holdings, the top-ranked stocks and the day's big movers, rates each story's sentiment, recomputes your portfolio, refreshes this page, and sends a push notification when something important happens.

The score

  • Momentum (20%): 12-month return, skipping the last month, plus 6-month return.
  • Quality (20%): operating and net profit margins, low debt to equity, and return on equity where it is reported.
  • Value (15%): earnings yield (1/PE) and book yield (1/PB).
  • Growth (15%): revenue and earnings growth.
  • Trend (10%): price vs the 200-day average, and 50-day above 200-day average.
  • Low volatility (10%): lower 1-year volatility scores higher.
  • News sentiment (10%): the agent's reading of recent news, fading over a few days.

Each factor is a z-score within the stock's sector, so a bank is compared with banks, not with IT firms. Extreme values are trimmed. Missing data counts as neutral and lowers the coverage figure. "Liquid" means a median daily traded value of at least ₹2 crore over 60 days.

Portfolio measures

Beta, alpha (Jensen's), Sharpe, Sortino, tracking error, information ratio, upside and downside capture, maximum drawdown, Calmar, and 1-day 95% VaR and CVaR. All are computed from daily returns of your current holdings at today's weights over the last 252 trading days. XIRR uses your actual buy dates and prices. Sector weights for the Nifty 500 are approximated from full market cap, while the official index uses free-float.

Limits

Data comes from free public sources. It can be delayed by about 15 minutes, miss a stock, or fail for an hour. Yahoo's fundamentals can lag company filings. This is a research tool. It ranks and explains; it does not tell you to buy or sell, and it is not investment advice. Check anything important against the exchange or your broker before acting.
